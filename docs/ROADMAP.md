# WideOrder release roadmap

## Phase 0.1 ? functional foundation

Target release: **v0.1.0004**. Secure administrator settings, configurable SMTP, native inquiry capture, encrypted durable queue, contact navigation consolidation and duplication audit. Keep the approved version ledger, branding, four palettes and appearance selector. SMTP account setup and mailbox delivery remain pending administrator credentials. No phase advance is implied by deployment.

## Mandatory Phase 0.2 handoff ? documentation only

The previously approved **?PROPOSED CASE STUDY PRESENTATION?** is the required reference. The requirements below preserve the supplied design direction; the existing simple comparisons are not a replacement for that reference. Do not implement this redesign until Phase 0.2 is authorized. Its first release is **v0.2.0001**, allocated by the established phase policy.

- Bold side-by-side **BEFORE** and **AFTER** panels with clear visual contrast.
- Large outcome measurements and a prominent improvement metric.
- Complex workflow visualization on the BEFORE side; clean, simplified workflow visualization on the AFTER side.
- Desktop hover, mobile touch, keyboard accessibility and reduced-motion support.
- Reusable comparison components based on this visual treatment across the remaining case studies.

Flagship: **Month-end reporting**.

| Before | After | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 20 hours | 10 minutes | Approximately **99.2% less active human preparation time** |

The calculation is `(1,200 - 10) / 1,200 ? 100 ? 99.2%`. It describes active human preparation, not elapsed completion time or independent background processing. Keep that distinction explicit. Do not present the result as a universal performance guarantee.
