## Interpretation rules

**THESE FILES ARE VISUAL/LAYOUT REFERENCES.**

They establish direction for:

- overall page composition
- broad horizontal layouts
- whitespace
- page density
- section rhythm
- card proportions
- alignment
- component restraint
- visual hierarchy
- light-theme atmosphere
- dark-theme atmosphere
- button treatment
- section surfaces
- border treatment
- calm geometry
- overall level of polish

They are **not**:

- sources of website copy
- sources of service names
- sources of marketing claims
- instructions to add photography
- instructions to invent dashboard graphics
- instructions to reproduce placeholder content

The eventual website should combine:

- approved content from `docs/CONTENT.md`
- visual/layout thinking from the two reference images

## Core principle

"The final site should look as though the WideOrder reference layouts were originally designed around the approved WideOrder Resolve content."

Do not simply recolor the previous GreenLangton layout.

Do not reproduce placeholder content visible in the reference images.
