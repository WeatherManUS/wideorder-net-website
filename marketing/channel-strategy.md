# WideOrder channel strategy

The website remains the authoritative source for approved messaging, case-study facts, and enquiry destinations. Use the master brand identity in `brand/brand-guidelines.md`: social channels vary their composition, not their identity. All plans below are an editorial operating model, not claims about account availability or guaranteed marketing performance.

## Channel roles

| Priority | Channel | Primary role | Reusable material | Destination and measurement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | LinkedIn | Executive credibility, practical operational insights, business-to-business enquiries | Company identity, problem post, before/after case study, document sequence, result announcement, article cover, sponsored creative | Link to a relevant case study or service page with `utm_source=linkedin`; evaluate permitted visits and successful enquiries, not likes as leads |
| 1 | YouTube | Explain problems and demonstrate how the outcome was achieved | Channel art, thumbnail, title/intro/outro cards, lower thirds, recording overlays, before/after transition, end screen, vertical title graphic | Relevant website case-study URL in description, tagged `youtube`; keep native video engagement separate from website engagement |
| 2 | Facebook | Reinforce recognition and distribute accessible problem/result stories | Profile/cover, square/portrait posts, short-video graphics, case-study promotion, ads when authorized | Separate `facebook` source, even when an ad is managed through Meta |
| 2 | Instagram | Recognition through concise visual storytelling | Profile mark, square/portrait posts, carousels, Reels covers, Stories, before/after graphics | Separate `instagram` source; adapt text and crop rather than shrink a LinkedIn graphic |
| 3 | X | Short operational observations and compact case-study summaries | Profile/header, landscape/square image post, campaign cards | `x` source; retain factual qualifiers and link to the full context |
| Extension | Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, newsletters, permitted Reddit placements, Google Business Profile if applicable | Reuse the same rules where the audience and platform fit | Choose a placement-specific preset and check current upload rules | Add a documented source/medium; no automatic account creation, scraping, or ad activation |

## Repeating content sequence

Start from approved website content. A lightweight sequence can consist of:

1. **The Problem:** one recognizable operational burden, a complexity visual, and a question that leads to the relevant page.
2. **Before and After:** the supported starting state and improved state with clear labels. Only show measured numbers already approved in the source case study.
3. **The Insight:** a practical observation supported by the existing methodology, without inventing a client quotation or promising a universal result.
4. **The Case Study:** problem → approach → outcome, followed by a link to the detailed account.
5. **The Demonstration:** a screen recording or process explanation, with a short opening problem and the actual outcome. Remove private client data before recording.

Choose frequency based on available production/review capacity. Reuse the research and facts across channels, but rewrite captions and recompose frames for the placement. Never imply that one case-study result is an expected result for every client.

## Release workflow

1. Select the approved page/case-study identifier and template. Verify wording and measurements against `docs/CONTENT.md` and the site content module.
2. Generate a placement-specific export and inspect it against [platform requirements](platform-requirements.md). Confirm commercial font notices, supplied logo use, contrast, crop, and legibility at thumbnail size.
3. Build a campaign URL using the repository's UTM utility. Record destination, channel, campaign, creative identifier, reviewer, and publication date in the campaign register.
4. Have an authorized account owner verify profile ownership and publish manually. Check accessibility text/captions, link destination, and platform preview before release.
5. Review engagement and enquiry results using the [reporting specification](reporting-specification.md), distinguishing actual observations from modeled estimates.

No account handles are assumed. Add a website social link only after an owner confirms that the profile is active, belongs to WideOrder, and is intended for public display. Advertising access, budgets, billing, conversion IDs, and approvals are separate setup tasks.

## Account-side checklist

- Confirm company identity, administrators, recovery methods, and multi-factor authentication using each platform's account tools.
- Upload the appropriate identity assets and test desktop/mobile crops.
- Supply verified profile URLs through the site's supported configuration; leave unavailable channels absent.
- For paid activity, authorize the ad account and select a specific conversion action before enabling its integration. Do not count a mailto/CTA click as a qualified enquiry.
- Approve destination copy and privacy information before releasing a campaign. Consent-gated analytics must remain optional; rejecting tracking must not prevent reading the site or initiating contact.

The technical configuration steps and current vendor integration references belong in `tracking-integrations.md`. No publication or account activation is part of this repository setup.
